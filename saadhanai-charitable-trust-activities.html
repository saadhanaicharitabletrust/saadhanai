Our Activities

Working together to make a difference in every aspect of life

Basic Activities

  • To establish institutions to promote peace, patriotism, and scientific awareness.
  • Cooperate with the government to eradicate poverty, social injustice, and illiteracy.
  • Support tribal communities with meticulous care.
  • Provide shelters for orphans, widows, and elderly people.
  • Partner with organizations like W.H.O. and UNICEF for assistance.
  • Collect and manage donations for charitable purposes.
  • Promote unity and mutual fraternity in society.

Medical Activities

  • Provide free medical camps, eye care, and blood donation drives.
  • Offer free medicines and ambulance services for the poor.
  • Establish hospitals and research centers.
  • Support people with physical disabilities with mobility aids.
  • Provide special training for mentally and physically challenged individuals.

Educational Activities

  • Form a group of intellectuals to guide aspiring students.
  • Preserve ancient manuscripts, scripts, and universal knowledge.
  • Organize free skill-spotting programs for the underprivileged.
  • Establish educational institutions from kindergarten to higher education.
  • Encourage extracurriculars like sports, music, yoga, and meditation.

Financial Activities

  • Provide financial help to widows and orphans for maintenance and education.
  • Promote skill development through vocational training and craft learning.
  • Support institutions with similar objectives through financial assistance.

Conferences

  • Organize seminars and conferences to derive practical solutions for societal growth.
  • Conduct workshops to engage in problem-solving discussions.

Nature Calamity Activities

  • Provide immediate relief during natural calamities like floods, droughts, and earthquakes.
  • Support affected communities with rehabilitation efforts.
  • Coordinate with local and international relief organizations for better outreach.
