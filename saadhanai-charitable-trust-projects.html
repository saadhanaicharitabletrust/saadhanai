Our Projects

Explore our ongoing and upcoming initiatives dedicated to making a difference.

Cancer Project - “Dhaya”

Supporting over 400+ cancer patients in Tamil Nadu and Kerala with the support of good-hearted people.

A Life Touched: Surya’s Story

On 16.12.2009, 7-year-old Surya, a handicapped child, underwent open heart surgery at K.G. Hospital, Coimbatore.

Through financial support from the Tamil Nadu government and contributions from kind-hearted individuals, 80% of the costs were covered.

Saadhanai Elders Home

Saadhanai Elders Home accommodates elderly individuals with a non-payable system, offering care and support.

Upcoming: Saadhanai Child Home

Helping Manikandan Walk Again

Saadhanai Charitable Trust has sponsored an artificial leg for Manikandan, helping him regain mobility and confidence.
