About Saadhanai

Download Brochure

SAADHANAI CHARITABLE TRUST was founded on September 28th, 2009, with the primary mission of serving underprivileged communities. The belief that has continually inspired Saadhanai is, "We will join hands and march together for the progress of our Nation."

The organization is headquartered and actively operates from Sivanandapuram, Coimbatore, Tamilnadu, the Trust continues to operate with the goal of making a meaningful impact in the lives of underprivileged individuals.

The Man Behind Saadhanai

It all started when the founder Randheer.B.Kurup lost his leg in an accident. His life all of sudden became stand still and tough to lead and that’s when he realised that even to do basic things he needed the help of somebody. This situation made him look beyond himself and made him realise that there are so many physically, mentally and financially challenged people who are in the same situation and this realisation of how difficult it would be for them to lead their life without other's help.

This experience ignited a desire to support the less fortunate, leading to the formation of Saadhanai Charitable Trust. Randheer’s unwavering commitment continues to inspire the Trust's mission to serve the community.

As the saying goes, "One hand doesn't make a sound.", so we join our hands together to uplift the challenged people. Saadhanai is always open to your valuable advices, support and cooperation to enable us extend our best effort for the betterment of the community and society.

Our Vision

  • To ensure that every underprivileged child attains his or her right to education, health, and opportunity.
  • Educate the needy for a better tomorrow and dream to see a society with moral values.

Our Mission

  • To educate, empower, and enable every individual who wishes to make a positive difference in the life of the underprivileged child.
  • To uplift mankind & society through the pursuit of education, social services, and medical facilities at the highest level of excellence.
  • To support the public below the poverty line for their development in all necessary activities.
  • To ensure a better life and facilities in the difficult-to-reach deep interiors of the country.
