About Us

Saadhanai Charitable Trust is a non-governmental, non-profitable social service organization established in September 2009. Our mission is to provide assistance to the poor, sick, and disabled, particularly those abandoned by society. We believe in extending our hands to those in need, offering education for underprivileged children, conducting health camps, and running social awareness programs.

"Lead Kindly Light" - Together, with your support, we can continue to make a difference.

Our Mission

Our primary focus is to uplift the economically and socially backward people in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Many individuals are suffering in poverty and ignorance, unaware of what tomorrow holds.

This harsh reality drove us to act. With determination and dedication, we formed "Saadhanai Charitable Trust." Though financial challenges remain, our vision is unwavering — to bring light into the lives of those in darkness.

Admissions Open for Elderly People

Our new initiative is a dedicated home for the elderly and destitute. In a serene environment surrounded by greenery, we provide clean accommodation, nutritious meals, medical care, and compassionate support.

No one should feel abandoned. Here, they will find a home that respects their dignity and cares for their well-being.
